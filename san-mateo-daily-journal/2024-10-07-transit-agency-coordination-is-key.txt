 Transit agency coordination is key 

 Editor,

 My household relies on Caltrain, SamTrans and BART for our daily transportation needs. These transit options are essential for me and my wife’s respective commutes to work, for us to patronize local businesses, and for us to visit family and friends around the Bay Area.

 That’s why I strongly support funding efforts to keep these transit services running, while also advocating for more frequent and better-connected routes. Reliable public transportation doesn’t just benefit my family — it’s crucial for reducing traffic congestion for all, improving the region’s air quality, and ensuring that everyone — regardless of income — has access to affordable mobility.

 We need our community’s leaders to come together to find solutions to address the looming fiscal cliff threatening these services. A coordinated effort between our region’s transit agencies is critical to avoid huge cuts that could make it harder for people to get where they need to go.

 If we don’t act, we risk losing the backbone of the Bay Area’s transportation network, which would have serious consequences for individuals, families and the economy as a whole. Public transportation is an investment in a more sustainable, connected future.

 Max Mautner

 San Mateo
